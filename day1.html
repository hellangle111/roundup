English Grammar Quiz

1. Put the words in order: (you / going to / What / do / are / tomorrow?)

2. Tom is ________ (tall) than his younger sister.

3. Fill in the blank with in / on / at: My birthday is ________ October 15th.

4. Choose the correct word: (Why / Where / How) is your backpack? – It’s under the desk.

5. I wanted to play football, ________ it started to rain heavily.

6. Are ________ your shoes over there near the door?

7. Sarah ________ (have got) two cats and a goldfish.

8. Find and correct the mistake: Look! The cat sleeps under the tree.

9. Write the plural form of the word: bus

10. Complete with How much or How many: ________ sugar do you need for the cake?

11. Last Sunday, we ________ (visit) our grandparents in the country.

12. They ________ (not go) to the beach yesterday because it was cold.

13. Put the words in order: (open / Please / window / don't / the)
