🔥 Present Tenses – Level 3 (V3)

⚠️ Be careful with meaning, not just time words!
1. She (need) your help.
2. We (prepare) for the test right now.
3. He (seem) tired today.
4. Why (you / wear) a jacket now?
5. I (agree) with you.
6. The children (make) a lot of noise at the moment.
7. She usually (remember) my birthday.
8. What (he / want)?
9. Look! The sky (get) dark.
10. This book (cost) a lot of money.
