English Grammar Practice

Choose: have, has, am, is, are

Exercise 1: A Big Family Day

This my friend Leo. He a very big family. There eight people in his family.
Leo two brothers and three sisters. His parents busy but very kind.
His father a teacher. He tall and strong. His mother a nurse. She long black hair.
Leo nine years old and he in grade three. He a blue school bag and two notebooks.
His brothers twins. They six years old. They many toy cars.
Leo and his sisters happy together. They a dog and a cat at home.

Exercise 2: My Friends at School

I a lot of friends at school. My best friend Mia. She ten years old and she very friendly.
Mia short hair and glasses. She good at math.
Tom and Ben in my class. They brothers. They the same bike.
Our teacher very nice. She a red dress today.
We many lessons every day. English our favorite subject.
At break time, we fun together. We very happy students.
