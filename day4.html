🔥 Present Simple & Continuous – Level 3 (V2)

⚠️ Careful with meaning, not only time words!
1. I (believe) you.
2. She (look) for her phone right now.
3. This bag (belong) to me.
4. Why (they / shout) now?
5. He usually (prefer) tea to coffee.
6. We (think) about your idea at the moment.
7. She (have) a new bicycle.
8. What (she / want)?
9. The children (swim) in the pool now.
10. Water (freeze) at 0°C.
